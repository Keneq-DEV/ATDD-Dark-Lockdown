# Dark-Lockdown

Dark-Lockdown is a mod for Amnesia: The Dark Descent set in the SCP Foundation.

The mod brings Amnesia's first-person survival horror into a world of anomalous
objects, restricted facilities, and strict containment procedures. When a
lockdown turns a controlled environment into a place of uncertainty, isolation
and danger, every corridor can feel unfamiliar and every step forward carries
new tension.

Dark-Lockdown explores the unsettling contrast between the Foundation's
attempts to control the unknown and the fear of facing it alone. It is an
atmospheric horror experience built around suspense, mystery, and the
ever-present threat of what may be waiting beyond the next door.
